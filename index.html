Hi! I’m a freelance 3D generalist, mapmaker, and educator based in Portugal.

My work spans a wide range of subjects and technologies, with a particular focus on 3D and animation, cartography and GIS, and the use of digital tools for cultural heritage. I bring extensive experience in higher education and professional training. I am also an active contributor to several Free/Libre & Open Source projects and an OpenStreetMap Foundation Active Contributor Associate Member.

You can explore more of my professional and artistic activities through Project Alfobre and Balborinho studio.
